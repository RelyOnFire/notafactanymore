“Fixed star” was originally a useful contrast with the visibly wandering planets, not necessarily a claim that stars could never move in three-dimensional space.

Proper motion is angular motion across the sky. Radial velocity measures motion toward or away from us, and both are needed to reconstruct a star's full space velocity.
