Some conditions involving fracture, severe neurological compromise, systemic illness, or postoperative instructions require temporary restriction. Those are not ordinary nonspecific back pain.

“Remain active” does not mean ignoring severe pain or immediately resuming every load. Activity can be modified and increased gradually.
