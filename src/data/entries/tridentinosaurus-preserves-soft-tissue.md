Found in the Italian Alps in 1931, the specimen offered an unusually complete-looking outline of an early reptile. Paolo Leonardi's 1959 description interpreted the dark material as carbonized soft tissue. Such preservation is possible, although this specimen's appearance puzzled researchers.

Valentina Rossi and colleagues tested the material in 2024. Microscopy and chemical measurements identified manufactured pigment, including bone black, in the outline. Its apparent anatomy could no longer carry the same evidential weight.

Some hindlimb bones appeared genuine. The correction therefore concerned what the surface preserved and which conclusions it supported. Who altered the specimen, when, and with what intention remain unresolved.
