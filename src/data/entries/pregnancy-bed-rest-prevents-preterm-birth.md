Individual patients may need temporary restrictions for a specific condition, hospitalization, procedure, or symptom. That is different from prescribing prolonged bed rest as a general preventive treatment.

The evidence base contains relatively few large randomized trials, but absence of demonstrated benefit matters when the intervention itself has known harms.
