Historically, physicians distinguished supposedly healthy thick pus from foul or spreading infection. The old position was therefore more specific than the claim that every pus-filled wound was good.

Some sterile inflammatory processes can also produce pus-like material. Its presence is a clinical sign requiring interpretation, not proof of one particular organism.
