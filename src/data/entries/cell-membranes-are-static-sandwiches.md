The 1972 fluid-mosaic model was itself a framework, not a final literal picture. Modern membranes contain crowded protein complexes, lipid domains, extracellular attachments, and cytoskeletal barriers that constrain movement.

The superseded element is the universal rigid sandwich, not the lipid bilayer or the observation that proteins occur on both membrane surfaces.
