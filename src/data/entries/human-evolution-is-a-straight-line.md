The linear image was never the only evolutionary model, and Darwinian branching was understood long before the fossil record became extensive. The superseded claim concerns the common scientific and educational practice of arranging a small set of fossils as a progressive sequence leading directly to modern humans.

Branching does not mean that ancestry is unknowable. Some populations were ancestral to later ones, but many named species were cousins, side branches, or overlapping populations rather than evenly spaced rungs.
