Hypnosis can have legitimate clinical uses, including pain and anxiety treatment. This card concerns memory accuracy, not hypnosis as a whole.

Some forgotten experiences can later be remembered accurately, but hypnosis cannot reliably distinguish them from suggestion or confabulation.
