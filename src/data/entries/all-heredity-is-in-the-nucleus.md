Most genes in eukaryotes are nuclear, and many proteins needed by mitochondria and chloroplasts are encoded in the nucleus. The correction is therefore a narrowing, not a reversal of chromosome theory.

Patterns of organelle inheritance also vary: maternal transmission is common, but paternal, biparental, and occasional leakage occur in different organisms.
